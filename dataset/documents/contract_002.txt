CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Consulting Agreement ("Agreement") is made on March 1, 2024, between Global Industries Inc. ("Company"), located at 500 Corporate Blvd, Chicago, IL 60601, and Maria Rodriguez ("Consultant"), residing at 300 Oak Avenue, Denver, CO 80202.

1. ENGAGEMENT. Company engages Consultant to provide strategic advisory services for the Q2 2024 product launch.

2. COMPENSATION. Company shall pay Consultant $150 per hour, not to exceed $25,000 total. Invoices submitted bi-weekly.

3. DELIVERABLES. Consultant shall provide: (a) Market analysis report by March 15, 2024; (b) Go-to-market strategy by April 1, 2024; (c) Weekly status updates.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY. Consultant agrees to maintain strict confidentiality of all Company proprietary information.

5. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR. Consultant is an independent contractor, not an employee. No benefits provided.

6. TERM. This Agreement runs from March 1, 2024 to June 30, 2024.

7. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate with 14 days written notice.

8. GOVERNING LAW. Illinois law governs this Agreement.
